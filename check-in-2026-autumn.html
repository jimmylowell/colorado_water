Method

How the figures are made

  • Reservoirs: Colorado DWR CDSS telemetry of 23 November, summed over the reservoirs that reported that day, against each one’s median for that week (daily records from 2005). A reservoir with no reading is left out.
  • Percent full: storage over the capacity in the site’s reservoir list (Data).
  • Animas: USGS 09361500, provisional discharge at 16:30 on 23 November, against its weekly median (USGS daily values from 1991).
  • Lake Powell: USBR Upper Colorado daily storage and elevation, provisional, read 23 November.
  • Snow: the fixed panel of 43 NRCS SNOTEL sites of the snowpack by decade, each against its own median for that day, water years 1981 to 2026.
  • File: data/live.json, generated 24 November 2026 00:03 UTC.
  • Correction: the about page of 10 November gave the Rio Grande near Del Norte at 875 cfs. That was USGS’s last value for the gage, from 30 April 2003. It is left out here.
