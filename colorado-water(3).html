How to read it

Solid ring — a measured, dated reading
Dashed ring — estimated from its basin's % of median
Bone tick — where the level normally sits today
Dashed ribbon — a tunnel carrying water under the Divide

Disc area is total capacity. Fill colour is the water's source; fill height is storage. The % figure under each disc is condition against the 1991–2020 median.

The seven colours

Colours repeat only between rivers that can never mix, so within any one river every colour is unique and a blend is always traceable. Braid mode splits every ribbon into its true source shares — nothing overlaps; the widths are the arithmetic.
