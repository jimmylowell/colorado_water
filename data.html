How every number is graded

Each displayed value carries a method — how much to trust it, and where it came from:

  • measured A published or telemetered reading from the operator of the dam or gage (USGS, Colorado DWR, USBR, Denver Water). Taken as-is.
  • derived Arithmetic on measured values: percent of normal = a current reading ÷ that same station's median for this week of the year. The medians are baked once from each station's own long record (see the ledger below), not guessed.
  • estimated No same-day public reading, so storage is scaled to the basin's reported percent of median. A fair guess, flagged as one.
  • schematic The map geometry and the flow-and-mixing model — a deliberately simplified diagram of where water goes, not survey data.

Column dictionary

  • capacity_af / storage_af — total and current storage in acre-feet. An acre-foot floods one acre a foot deep: roughly what two Front Range households use in a year.
  • pct_of_median — storage against this station's median for this calendar week, 100 = a normal year. When live, it is derived from the reading and the baked median; otherwise it carries the operator's or basin's reported figure.
  • confidence / method — observed / measured: a published, dated reading; live / derived: DWR telemetry fetched by your browser, compared to the baked median; basin estimate / estimated: scaled from the basin's percent of median.
  • role — water supply, or flood control for the Corps of Engineers pools (Cherry Creek, Bear Creek): famous lakes nobody drinks from, whose capacity_af is just the small permanent pool and whose percent-of-median is blank because "normal storage" doesn't apply.
  • dwr_telemetry_abbrev — Colorado DWR station code, usable directly against the CDSS station tools and REST API.

The source ledger

Everything traces to a keyless, CORS-open, public-domain endpoint. Histories are pulled once and reduced to weekly day-of-year medians by scripts/build_normals.py, which writes js/normals.js; the browser then fetches only current values and derives the percentages live.
